# PBI-053: Durable artifact storage (volumes + env-driven roots)

## Directive

Stop losing artifacts on redeploy (renders today, boards/uploads
tomorrow):

1. Env-driven roots with repo-relative defaults (offline gates
   unaffected): `SWE_DRIP_RUNS_DIR` (default `runs/`) consumed by
   `api/app/runs.py::runs_root()` + render nodes + designs router, and
   `SWE_DRIP_COLLECTIONS_DIR` (default `collections/`) consumed by the
   collections store + PBI-049 ingest. No hardcoded `runs/` or
   `collections/` paths may remain on these flows (grep-guarded in
   tests).
2. `docker-compose.yml` + Coolify: persistent volume mounts for both
   roots on `swe-drip-api` (documented exact mount paths), wired to the
   env vars above.
3. `DEPLOY.md` — storage section: which paths persist, backup note
   (Coolify volume snapshots), and the local-dev equivalence (defaults
   just work).
4. Tests: roots resolve from env (monkeypatched) and fall back to
   defaults; a grep-guard test fails on hardcoded `runs/` /
   `collections/` literals in the touched flows; migration notes that
   pre-volume artifacts are container-ephemeral (no backfill — old
   renders are already gone).

Scope boundaries: no new features (plumbing only); no backup automation
beyond documenting snapshots; no touching the checkpointer/audit stores.
