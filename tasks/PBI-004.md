## Directive

Add a lightweight `tests/deployment.test.js` that probes `https://swedrip.kcb.ma/health` (skippable when `DEPLOY_URL` is empty), extend `scripts/lint.js` or add `scripts/verify-deploy.js` to assert `Dockerfile`/`nginx.conf`/`.coolify/app.json` contracts, and write the redeploy/rollback/env-rotation/volume-backup runbook (per `docs/04-deploy.md` troubleshooting + `ARCHITECTURE.md:5` constraints).
